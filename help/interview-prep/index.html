How I can help

I've sat on both sides of the table many times, as the candidate and as the one asking the questions. We start from the role you're going for and run the interview as close to the real thing as I can: a system design round, a coding round in Java, or the behavioral questions. Afterwards you get written notes on what worked and what to fix, and we do the weakest part again.

What you get

  • A mock interview that feels like the real one
  • Written notes you can work from
  • Your CV and LinkedIn, read the way a hiring manager reads them
  • Prep material: the System Design Casebook and the 150 Java interview questions
