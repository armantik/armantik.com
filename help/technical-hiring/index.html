/how

How I can help

Many interview processes test what's easy to test instead of what the job needs, and lose good candidates on the way. I help you start from what the person will actually do, then design the stages, exercises and scorecards to match, including exercises that still tell you something now that candidates use AI tools every day. Then we run them with your interviewers so everyone scores the same way.

What you get

  • An interview plan from first call to offer
  • System design and coding exercises with clear scoring guides
  • A short guide for your interviewers
  • Exercises that still work when candidates use AI tools
