/mentoring/interviews

Preparing for interviews

For engineers going for mid level, senior or staff backend roles. We practice the interview you're actually facing, then go through what to fix.

  • A mock system design interview, with written notes afterwards
  • Coding interview practice in Java, thinking out loud and talking through tradeoffs
  • Your CV and LinkedIn, read the way a hiring manager reads them
  • Behavioral questions: telling the story of your projects clearly and briefly

To prepare on your own: the System Design Casebook has 14 case studies, the Senior Java Backend Guide has 150 interview questions with answers, and the algorithm guides solve classic problems several ways, step by step.

/mentoring/juniors

Growing as a developer

For developers in their first years, or moving into backend from another field. We set a direction together, then meet again to see how it's going.

  • A learning path through Java, data structures and how the JVM behaves
  • Code reviews: how to read them, how to write them, and how not to take them personally
  • Debugging, and finding your way around a production system
  • Working in a team: asking good questions, estimating, and speaking up in design discussions

A good place to start is the new joiners path in the Learning Lab.
