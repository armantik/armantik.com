Privacy

This site doesn't track you. Here is what does happen to your data, in plain words.

No cookiesNo analytics or trackersUpdated 1 November 2026

/site

When you visit the site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts. Everything, the fonts included, loads from armantik.com itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other third party while you read.
  • A few settings are saved in your own browser (local storage): your light or dark choice (aa-theme), the case studies you mark done in the System Design Casebook (sdc-done-v1), and the last view you picked in the LRU and LFU cache guide (cv-cache). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, and may keep them in its logs for security. I can't see those logs. GitHub explains what it does in its privacy statement.
  • Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Medium take you to those sites, and their own privacy rules apply there.

/contact

When you contact me

  • When you email me, call or text the number on my card, or message me on LinkedIn, I get your name, your contact details and whatever you choose to send, for example your CV, a LinkedIn link or a description of your team.
  • Please leave your personal identity number (personnummer), a photo and any health or other sensitive details out of your CV. I don't need them, and I delete them if they arrive.
  • I use it only to answer you and, if we agree to work together, to prepare and run our sessions. Your CV and my notes from our sessions stay in my own Google account. Apart from the services named on this page, I never share any of it, I never sell it, and I never add you to a mailing list.
  • Mail to my armantik.com address passes through Cloudflare's email routing, which forwards it for me, and lands in my personal Gmail account. Google stores and handles mail in Gmail under its own privacy policy. Calls and texts stay on my phone, and LinkedIn messages stay on LinkedIn.
  • GitHub, Cloudflare and Google are US companies, so your data may be processed in the United States. Those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.
  • I keep emails, CVs and session notes for up to 24 months after we were last in touch, so we can pick up where we left off if you come back for your next job search. Then I delete them. Tell me if you'd like them deleted sooner, or kept longer for a later round. If you pay me for a session, the invoice is kept for seven years, because Swedish bookkeeping law requires it.
  • The legal basis is that you asked me for something (steps before an agreement, GDPR article 6(1)(b)), or my legitimate interest in answering the messages people send me (article 6(1)(f)). Invoices are kept to meet a legal obligation (article 6(1)(c)).

/rights

Your rights

  • You can ask for a copy of what I have about you, ask me to correct it or delete it, ask me to limit what I do with it, or ask for what you sent me in a form you can take elsewhere. Email me and I'll answer within a month.
  • Your right to object. Where I rely on my legitimate interest, for example keeping messages you sent me, you can object at any time. I'll then stop and delete them, unless we're preparing sessions you asked for.
  • If you think I've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.
