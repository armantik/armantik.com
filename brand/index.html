Identity

The colors, type and mark used on armantik.com. Use the same values for the business card, slides and social images so everything reads as one person.

/brand/mark

The mark

A geometric A in a rounded square. The file is assets/favicon.svg. Keep clear space of a quarter of its width on every side, and don't recolor it outside the palette.

/brand/rules

Rules

  • One accent per layout: petrol. No gradients, no second bright color.
  • Ruled lines and whitespace instead of boxes and shadows.
  • Mono for anything that behaves like data: dates, paths, labels, code.
  • No dashes as punctuation in copy; use commas, colons or "to" for ranges.
  • No employer logos. Names in text only, with public information only.
