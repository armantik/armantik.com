/about

Twenty years of backends where mistakes are expensive

I've been building backend systems since 2006. My first fifteen years were in Iran: internet and mobile banking channels used by several banks, a cryptocurrency exchange, and a fintech platform where I led the move from a PHP monolith to Java and Go services handling payments, credit and top ups.

Since 2021 I've worked in Sweden. At Klarna in Stockholm I helped design and build an automated Right to be Forgotten platform and worked on a decision engine used across the company. Today I'm in Malmö, working on the backend behind Apple's Find My.

What ties it together is systems where correctness, privacy and availability are not optional. In my own time I work on the newer half of modern backends, putting LLMs to work inside real services with MCP, RAG, agents and proper evaluation.

/now

Now building: Kairos

In progress · personal research

Kairos reads unstructured financial news and turns it into scored, structured signals. It combines LLMs with structured outputs, retrieval over a vector store, tool-using agents connected through MCP, and an evaluation harness that keeps the scores honest. Built in Java and Python.

A research project, not investment advice. Happy to talk about the architecture.
